Glass Run Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

In short: Glass Run does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and does not use the internet. Your game progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Glass Run is an Android game made by BoidBit ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal information. The game has no sign-in and no user accounts.

What is stored on your device

To remember where you were, the game saves a small file on your phone's own storage. It holds only:

  • your best distance, your latest checkpoint, and your Level 1 result and stars;
  • your settings for sound, music, vibration and graphics quality.

This file never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the game's data in Android settings. Automatic cloud backup of the game's data is turned off.

Permissions

The game asks only for permission to vibrate your phone, so that glass breaking can be felt. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings. The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Internet, ads and third parties

The game does not connect to the internet, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking tools from us or anyone else. Because nothing is sent anywhere, no information is shared with third parties.

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your use of Play under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive personal information from Google.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding ads or online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/boidbit/glass-garden/issues.
