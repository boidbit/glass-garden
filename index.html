How to play

  1. Aim and shoot. Tap the glass to fire the slingshot. Hold to keep firing. Drag to look around.
  2. Free play. Walk with the stick (or WASD), switch to the ball, the hammer or an earthquake on the right. Nothing is lost: Rewind puts every piece back.
  3. Garden Run. The path moves on its own. Break the glass in your way before you reach it, hit the glowing gems for pebbles, and keep a streak going for double and triple shots.
  4. The sky. After each pavilion you fly up into the clouds. Up there the glass moves: shoot the glowing hub of a spinning fan, break the scissors before they shut, the serpent's head, the pane a storm cloud is charging. Thick green glass takes two or three hits. Fly through the rings for pebbles and speed.
  5. Checkpoints. Bring down the glass pavilion before each checkpoint for a slow-motion finish and ten pebbles. If you run out, carry on from your last checkpoint.
  6. Breathe mode. Turn it on in the pause menu. Hold your finger down and breathe in as the ring grows, then let go to breathe out and break the glass.
