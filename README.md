# Kaanch Ka Kamra — Glass Garden

A calm, stress-relief glass-breaking game in the browser, made with three.js (r170) and cannon-es.

- **Free play:** cosy rooms full of glass to break with a slingshot, ball or hammer. Rewind puts everything back.
- **Glass Run:** an endless path through a garden, jungle, mountains and a river. Each zone has its own glass (stained-glass windows, panes on vines, ice walls, a glass lotus and more), with a glass pavilion to bring down before every checkpoint.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (it loads three.js and cannon-es from jsDelivr, so it needs an internet connection), or serve the folder:

```
npx serve .
```

Graphics can be set to Auto, Low, Medium or High in the settings. Low is lightest for phones.
